# MVP Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Discovery
- Confirm authoritative compliance-data owner and approved integration.
- Confirm fields, SLAs, legal/privacy basis and sample data.
- Field-test plate capture conditions at pump stations and roadside.

## Phase 1 — Core verification
- Auth/RBAC.
- Camera upload.
- OCR/ANPR.
- Normalization/manual correction.
- Mock/approved compliance provider.
- Rule engine.
- Result/alert screen.
- Audit trail.

## Phase 2 — Operations
- Verification history.
- Supervisor/manual-review queue.
- Offline-tolerant capture.
- Operational metrics and alerts.
- Admin rules/provider configuration.

## Phase 3 — Enterprise scale
- Multi-region/DR as required.
- MDM/device posture.
- Advanced fraud/evidence controls.
- Provider reconciliation.
- Data lifecycle automation.
- Performance optimization from production telemetry.
